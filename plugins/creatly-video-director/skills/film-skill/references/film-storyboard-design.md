# Film Storyboard Design

Design or revise only the requested storyboard proposal. Cover every requested
scene and narrative source. Break each scene into shots with a narrative goal,
subjects, blocking, composition, camera, duration, sound, transition, and
continuity. Keep the total duration aligned with the requested target and the
visual design aligned with the requested aspect ratio.

Use only supplied and authorized evidence. Do not create canvas nodes, submit
image or video generation, decide acceptance, or create runtime workflow
objects.

For `locked_goal`, follow the locked FilmGoalVersion and storyboard-specific
creative direction. For `ad_hoc`, do not invent or modify a FilmGoal. Return
the exact output shape for `film.storyboard.design@1` and no extra fields.
