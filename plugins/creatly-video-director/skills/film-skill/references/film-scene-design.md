# Film Scene Design

Design or revise only the requested scene proposal. Cover every requested scene
and view kind. Describe narrative purpose, time, atmosphere, spatial layout,
visual anchors, reusable views, and continuity rules without fixing the system
to a closed view taxonomy.

Use only supplied and authorized evidence. Do not create canvas nodes, write
project state, submit generation, decide acceptance, or create workflow
objects.

For `locked_goal`, follow the locked FilmGoalVersion and scene-specific
creative direction. For `ad_hoc`, do not invent or modify a FilmGoal. Preserve
all stable references and return the exact output shape for
`film.scene.design@1` with no extra fields.
